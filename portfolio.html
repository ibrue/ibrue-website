Smart Glasses Hardware

From concept to mass manufacturing in Shenzhen.

RoleHardware design
StatusPatented · Manufactured
ToolsFusion 360, DFM
OutputProduction tooling

Problem

Smart glasses live or die on weight, battery, and how they sit on a face. We needed an internal mounting system that could hold optics and electronics rigidly, survive drop tests, and still come apart cleanly on the assembly line in Shenzhen.

Approach

The piece you're rotating is a side-mount bracket — one of several iterations. It anchors the temple-arm electronics to the frame while isolating the optical module from flex. Designed around injection-molding draft, with snap features and locating bosses tuned for high-volume assembly.

Outcome

  • Concept → DFM → tooling → mass production
  • Co-inventor on patent for power-optimized image capture in smart wearables
  • Worked directly with manufacturing partners in Shenzhen
